A high-performance Persian speech recognition system built with PyTorch and distributed fine-tuning of Whisper across 3 GPUs, reducing the character error rate from 0.28 to 0.025.

The resulting Whisper models are open-source on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/ArashAzma) (1,200+ monthly downloads), released together with an automated audio annotation pipeline (ensembles of open-source AI models, 5,000+ hours curated), a benchmark dataset, and a leaderboard for reproducible Persian ASR evaluation.
