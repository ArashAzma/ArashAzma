I am a Computer Engineer (B.Sc.) at **Ferdowsi University of Mashhad (FUM)**, with research and professional experience in **artificial intelligence, large language models, speech processing, computer vision, and software development**. I am seeking a graduate opportunity to contribute to cutting-edge research and innovation in AI, robotics, and computer engineering.

## What I work on

* **Serverless function optimization** - collaborating with Dr. Saeid Abrishami on placing serverless cloud functions using graph neural network (HGT) embeddings that condition a Qwen3-8B LLM, trained with LoRA supervised fine-tuning and GRPO reinforcement learning against cost and latency objectives.
* **Persian speech recognition** - at [C1Tech Group](https://github.com/ArashAzma), distributed fine-tuning of Whisper across 3 GPUs (PyTorch) cut the character error rate from 0.28 to 0.025. The resulting Whisper models are open-source on Hugging Face (1,200+ monthly downloads), with an accompanying benchmark dataset and leaderboard for reproducible Persian ASR evaluation.
* **Vision research** - a plug-and-play convolutional attention module for improving spatial and channel-wise feature representation across diverse vision backbones, co-authored with a senior researcher and currently under review at a top-tier computer vision venue; plus human trajectory forecasting from aerial imagery (YNet, ADE 11.49 / FDE 20.23 for a 4-second horizon) and visual odometry (SIFT/ORB + PnP, benchmarked on KITTI).
