Persian text-to-speech models released at C1Tech Group, complementing the Persian speech recognition work.

* **VibeVoice Persian TTS** is a Persian fine-tune of Microsoft's VibeVoice-1.5B
* **OmniVoice Persian TTS** is a Persian fine-tune of k2-fsa's OmniVoice
* Both are released open-source on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/C1Tech) under the Apache-2.0 license
