A high-performance Persian speech recognition system built at C1Tech Group using PyTorch and distributed fine-tuning of Whisper across 3 GPUs, reducing the character error rate from 0.28 to 0.025.

* The resulting Whisper models are open-source on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/ArashAzma), reaching 1,200+ monthly downloads
* Released together with an accompanying benchmark dataset and leaderboard for reproducible Persian ASR evaluation
* Built on an automated audio annotation pipeline leveraging ensembles of open-source AI models, curating over 5,000 hours of high-quality speech data
