Conducted research within the Visual Odometry (VO) group, focusing on robust trajectory reconstruction.

* Developed an end-to-end VO pipeline utilizing feature extraction algorithms (SIFT and ORB) paired with Perspective-n-Point (PnP) for precise 3D-to-2D pose tracking
* Evaluated and benchmarked model performance using the KITTI autonomous driving vision dataset
* Researching a novel, plug-and-play convolutional attention module designed to enhance spatial and channel-wise feature representation across diverse vision backbones
