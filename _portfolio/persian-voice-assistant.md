Design and deployment of an end-to-end voice assistant integrating STT, LLM, and TTS modules with Node-RED-orchestrated AI agent workflows, enabling natural language requests to be translated into structured report-generation queries and dynamically executed multi-step workflows.

* Qwen and Gemma LLMs fine-tuned for intent detection and slot-filling, improving domain-specific intent classification accuracy by 18% (0.94 F1-score)
* Node-RED orchestration of AI agent workflows for dynamic, multi-step task execution
